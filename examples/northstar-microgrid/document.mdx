## The operating question

The system is not being judged on its best solar afternoon. It is being judged on **04:00 after two overcast days**, when the desalination pumps start and the hospital cannot notice the transition from battery to backup generation.

> **Commissioning threshold**
>
> Maintain all critical services with a loss-of-load probability below **0.5%**, while keeping annual diesel generation under **8%** of delivered energy.

## Commissioning decision

1. **Accept the electrical topology.** Protection coordination, black-start sequencing, and critical feeder transfer all passed witnessed tests.
2. **Add 1.2 MWh before winter year three.** Demand growth erodes the low-cloud reserve faster than the current operating plan acknowledges.
3. **Move desalination into the solar shoulder.** A two-hour schedule change removes the sharpest evening ramp without reducing water output.
4. **Retain generator G-02 through the first winter.** It contributes little annual energy but materially lowers tail risk during maintenance on battery bank B.

> The network is ready to enter service. Its next constraint is not generation capacity; it is when flexible demand chooses to run.
